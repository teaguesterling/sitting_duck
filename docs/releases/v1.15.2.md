# Sitting Duck v1.15.2

**Release date:** 2026-09-28

DuckDB v1.5.6 support.

Bumps the DuckDB line to v1.5.6, the community registry's stable line, and
fixes the compat shim that v1.5.6 broke by backporting the v2.0 parser-name
refactor **in pieces** — per-accessor probes plus a string/Identifier
normalizer, rather than one probe answering for several accessors.

Also raises the v2.0 test runner's per-batch budget so the four
ast_select-heavy suites fit it rather than being split or skipped.

Verified: full suite on v1.5.6 (7113 assertions, 133 cases); green on linux
amd64/arm64, macOS x86_64/arm64 and all three WASM targets.
